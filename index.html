A few things worth planning around.

Topiary Garden

Dozens of yew hedges clipped into the people, boats, and shoreline of Georges Seurat's A Sunday on La Grande Jatte — the only topiary version of a painting anywhere in the world, tucked into a downtown park most visitors walk right past.

Franklin Park Conservatory

An 1895 glass conservatory restored down to the ironwork, with permanent Dale Chihuly glass installations threaded through the biomes and a seasonal room full of live butterflies.

North Market

A public market that's been running since 1876, now a food hall of small vendors instead of butchers and dry-goods stalls. Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams opened its very first shop here.

COSI

A hands-on science museum on the riverfront that's built for kids but doesn't talk down to adults. Budget an afternoon — the big-format theater alone can eat an hour.

Planning the trip.

Getting here

John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH) sits about fifteen minutes from downtown. By car, Columbus sits at the crossing of I-70 and I-71, so it's rarely more than a few hours from anywhere in Ohio or its neighboring states.

Getting around

The core neighborhoods are walkable on their own, and CoGo Bike Share covers most of the gaps between them. For anything further out — Clintonville to German Village, say — plan on a car or rideshare.

Best time to visit

Fall brings Ohio State football Saturdays and the best weather for walking the neighborhoods. Summer means festival season. Spring is the Conservatory's live butterfly exhibit.
